Storyboard · Episode 01 of 30 · Week 1: Everyday Malaysia

Kalau Mamak Tiba-Tiba Hilang

What happens to Malaysia when every mamak stall disappears overnight? A 75-second flat-graphic animation. It starts with the funny late-night chaos, then turns into a question about where Malaysians actually meet each other.

Format: 9:16 verticalLength: ~75sVO: BM / Manglish, English subtitlesPlatform: TikTok · Reels · Shorts

Palette: a mamak at 2am

night tube light teh tarik red stool blue stool signboard

Graphic style

Flat vector shapes, no outlines, chunky signboard lettering for the on-screen text. Every scene is built from mamak objects: plastic stools, tube lights, steel cups and the yellow signboard.

Motion rules

  • Hook: things dissolve into dust
  • Text pops in word by word, synced to the VO
  • Loose, bouncy motion in Day 1, slow and still from Year 1 onward
  • Sound: mamak chatter that gets quieter through the episode
010:00–0:03Hook

The mamak dissolves

Visual
A busy mamak at 2am: signboard, tube lights, stools, cups. In one beat, everything breaks into dust from top to bottom and leaves an empty lot.
Motion
0.5s hold, then a 1.2s particle dissolve. "MAMAK HILANG." slams in with a small screen shake.
VO
"Bayangkan esok kau bangun… dan semua mamak kat Malaysia, hilang."Imagine you wake up tomorrow and every mamak in Malaysia is gone.
Sound
Loud chatter, the clink of a spoon in a cup, then a hard cut to silence.
020:03–0:08Hook

Group chat panic

Visual
A phone fills the screen. Chat bubbles stack up in the group chat, then the map search for "mamak near me" returns 0 results.
Motion
Bubbles bounce in on each notification sound. The "0" spins in like a slot machine.
VO
"Group chat mula panik. 'Jom mamak?'… tak ada jawapan."The group chat panics. "Mamak, anyone?"… no answer.
Sound
Three quick message pings, then a sad error beep.
030:08–0:20Day 1

Four small disasters

Visual
A 2×2 grid of mini scenes that pop in one by one: a football match at 3am, a study group, an empty Milo ais cup, a clock at 2am.
Motion
Quick, comedic cuts. Each tile wobbles in on the beat. The empty cup tips over.
VO
"Day 1. Match pukul 3 pagi, nak tengok kat mana? Study group, lepak mana? Milo ais, siapa nak buat?"Day 1. A 3am match: where do we watch? Study group: where do we sit? Milo ais: who's making it?
Sound
Upbeat percussion with a "boing" on each tile.
040:20–0:26Day 1

The pull snaps

Visual
Close-up of a teh tarik being pulled from cup to cup. The stream stretches longer and longer, then breaks in the middle.
Motion
Slow motion. The camera follows the stream down, and at the break the whole frame drops in saturation. This is the switch from funny to serious.
VO
"Tapi lama-lama, kita sedar… yang hilang bukan cuma teh tarik."But slowly we realise… it's not just the teh tarik we lost.
Sound
The music stops. Only the pouring sound, then one string note.
050:26–0:38Year 1

Everyone alone, together

Visual
The camera pulls back from one flat to a whole apartment block. In every window, one person sits alone with a blue phone glow. Then cut to a fancy new café: "Teh Tarik RM12".
Motion
Slow zoom out. The windows light up one at a time, all the same. The price tag drops in with a "ka-ching".
VO
"Year 1. Semua orang duduk rumah. Scroll. Sorang-sorang. Kafe baru buka, teh tarik dua belas ringgit. Tapi tak ada siapa borak dengan meja sebelah."Year 1. Everyone stays home, scrolling, alone. New cafés open with RM12 teh tarik, but nobody talks to the next table.
Sound
Low ambient hum and faint phone taps.
060:38–0:52Twist

The idea: a third place

Visual
A clean infographic. Circle 1 is home, circle 2 is work, and circle 3 is a dashed empty ring that slowly fills with a mamak stool icon.
Motion
The circles draw themselves in. The dashed lines connect them. Ring 3 pulses gently like a heartbeat.
VO
"Ahli sosiologi Ray Oldenburg panggil benda ni 'third place'. Rumah tempat pertama. Kerja tempat kedua. Tempat ketiga, tempat kita jadi sebahagian dari komuniti."Sociologist Ray Oldenburg calls this the "third place". Home is the first, work the second. The third is where we become part of a community.
Sound
Warm piano starts. A soft "ding" as each circle appears.
070:52–1:02Twist

One table, every drink

Visual
A top-down view of one mamak table with four stools. Four different drinks slide in: teh tarik, kopi O, bandung, Milo. Each cup stands for a different person. We never show faces, only the drinks.
Motion
Cups slide in from each side like hands placing them. Then a soft glow fills the table.
VO
"Kat meja mamak, tak ada orang tanya kau siapa. Teh tarik, kopi O, Milo, bandung, semua duduk satu meja."At a mamak table, nobody asks who you are. Every drink shares the same table.
Sound
Chatter slowly returns, warm and blurred.
081:02–1:10Tanya Balik

The question back

Visual
One empty red plastic stool under a spotlight. The question appears above it, and a "Komen bawah" (comment below) pill pops up at the bottom.
Motion
Complete stillness for 1 second, then the text types out slowly.
VO
"Jadi soalan aku: kalau mamak hilang, kau rindu makanan dia… atau orang-orang dia?"So my question: if mamak disappeared, would you miss the food… or the people?
Sound
Silence, then a single spoon tap on a glass.
